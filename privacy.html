# Bert Bot — Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** October 9, 2026 **Last Updated:** October 9, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Bert Bot is a Discord bot that provides automated welcome messages and quotes to Discord communities. This Privacy Policy explains how Bert Bot processes information while providing these features. ## 2. Information Processed Bert Bot temporarily processes limited Discord information necessary for its functionality, including: - Discord user IDs and usernames to identify and welcome new members. - Discord server and channel IDs to deliver messages. - Slash command interactions to respond to quote requests. Bert Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or sensitive personal information. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is used solely to: - Welcome new Discord server members. - Respond to quote commands. - Deliver messages to appropriate Discord channels. - Maintain the Bot's functionality. Bert Bot does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. ## 4. Data Storage and Retention Bert Bot does not maintain a permanent database of user information. Information received from Discord is processed temporarily to perform requested actions and is not intentionally retained after processing. Messages posted by Bert Bot may remain visible within Discord according to the platform's and server's settings. ## 5. Third-Party Services Bert Bot operates using Discord's API and may rely on hosting infrastructure to function. Discord independently handles information according to its own Privacy Policy. ## 6. Data Security Reasonable security practices are used to protect the Bot's credentials and infrastructure. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. ## 7. Data Access and Deletion Requests Users may contact the developer to inquire about information processed by Bert Bot or request deletion of any information controlled by the developer. Because Bert Bot does not maintain a permanent user database, there is ordinarily no stored personal information to delete. For privacy requests, contact: **thaliadwcpa@gmail.com** Messages already posted within Discord may need to be deleted through Discord's available tools. ## 8. Children's Privacy Bert Bot is intended for individuals eligible to use Discord under its applicable age requirements. The Bot does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. ## 9. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if Bert Bot's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be available through the published Privacy Policy URL. ## 10.
